The honest FAQ

Do we have to hire you to clean it up?

No. The score and roadmap are the product. Some clients clean up in-house, some hire us, some hire someone else. The plan is written so any competent team can execute it.

Is this safe?

Access is read-only and scoped. We evaluate data quality patterns, and access is revoked when we are done.

How is this different from Salesforce duplicate rules?

Duplicate rules catch one kind of problem, one record at a time. This scores your whole org across completeness, duplicates, and decay, and keeps watching so quality does not quietly slide back.

How is the price set?

By org size and complexity: record volume, object count, integrations. You get the number before we start, and it does not move.
