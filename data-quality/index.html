The honest FAQ

How is the Data Quality Score calculated?

Across completeness, duplicates, consistency, and decay, weighted by the business impact of each issue, so the score reflects real reliability, not just a raw error count.

Do we have to hire you to clean it up?

No. The score, insights, and prioritized path are the product. Some clients clean up in-house, some hire us, some hire someone else. The plan is written so any competent team can execute it.

How is this different from Salesforce duplicate rules?

Duplicate rules catch one kind of problem, one record at a time. This scores your whole org, prioritizes by business risk, and keeps monitoring in the background so quality does not quietly slide back.

Will this get us ready for AI?

That is a core part of it. Agentforce, Einstein, and generative AI are only as good as the data underneath. The platform surfaces the incomplete and fragmented records that compromise AI accuracy so you can fix them first.
