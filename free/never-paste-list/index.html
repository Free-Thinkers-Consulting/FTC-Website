My Never-PasteList

If you wouldn't put it on a billboard, don't put it in your chat.

Here's my never-paste list, plus what to do instead so you still get the help you need.

The list

Never paste these

01

Passwords and logins

That includes API keys, Wi-Fi passwords, and the shared login sitting in your team doc. If a task needs a login, do that part yourself.

02

Bank, card and tax ID numbers

Account numbers, card numbers, Social Security numbers and EINs. Need help with a statement or an invoice? Delete or black out the numbers first. The AI doesn't need them to help.

03

Client names in contracts

Swap in Client A, Client B before you paste. To change every name at once, use Find and replace: in Google Docs it's under Edit, and in Word it's Replace on the Home tab.

04

Employee health or HR details

Medical information, performance problems, pay disputes, or anything from a personnel file. Need help with a hard HR email? Describe the situation without names or health details.

Before you paste

Ask yourself 3 questions

05

The billboard check

Run through these before you hit paste:

  • Would I be okay if this showed up on a billboard?
  • Can I swap the names for Client A or Employee B?
  • Can I delete the numbers and still get the help I need?

Private chats

How to turn on private chats

06

In ChatGPT: Temporary chat

Open a new chat and select Temporary. Then choose Personalized (uses your memory and custom instructions) or Unpersonalized (uses neither) before your first message.

  • It stays out of your chat history unless you save it.
  • It isn't used to train models while it stays temporary, and it never creates memories.
  • OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety.

07

In Claude: Incognito chat

Start a new chat (outside a project) and click the ghost icon in the upper right corner. You'll see a black border and an Incognito chat label.

  • It isn't saved to your chat history or Claude's memory.
  • It isn't used to train models, even if you've turned that setting on.
  • Anthropic keeps it for 30 days by default. Once you close it, you can't reopen it.

08

Pasted something by mistake?

Delete the chat right away. Both companies say deleted chats are removed from their systems within 30 days, with a few exceptions.

  • ChatGPT: click ••• next to the chat, then Delete, and confirm.
  • Claude: hover over the chat in the sidebar, click ⋮, then Delete, and confirm.
  • If it was a password, change that password now.

09

Turn off training for every chat

Private chats are for one-offs. To keep all your chats out of training, switch it off in your settings. My ChatGPT settings guide shows where, and covers Claude too.

See the 5 ChatGPT settings to change →

A private chat still isn't the place for anything on the never-paste list. My rule: if you wouldn't put it on a billboard, don't put it in your chat.
