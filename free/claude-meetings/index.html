How to UseClaude Meetings

Claude can join your Google Meet calls. Here's who has it, how to add it, and what to ask it.

Here's the scoop on Claude Meetings.

Who has it

  1. Team and Enterprise plans. Claude Meetings is in beta on Claude Team and Enterprise plans, and it works with Google Meet.
  2. Your owner controls it. On Team plans it's on by default and an owner can turn it off. On Enterprise plans, an owner turns it on for all or part of the organization.
  3. Some setups can't use it yet. During the beta it isn't available to some organizations, including those set up for zero data retention, HIPAA, or customer-managed encryption keys.

Step by step

Add Claude to a call

01

Open Meetings in Claude

Go to Meetings in Claude, at claude.ai/meetings.

02

Connect Google Calendar

Connect your Google Calendar. An owner may need to allow Google Calendar for your organization first.

03

Add Claude to the meeting

Pick the meeting and click Add Claude. Claude joins the Google Meet call as a visible participant.

04

Optional: have it join on its own

To have Claude join automatically, go to Settings > Capabilities > Meetings > Auto-join.

Claude's guide: Use Claude Meetings ↗

05

Tell your guests first

Always tell your guests an AI is joining before the call.

During the call

What to ask it

06

Check what's agreed

Halfway through, or near the end, ask:

Prompt
Summarize what we agreed so far.

07

Get the action items

Before everyone drops off, ask:

Prompt
List the action items with owners.

08

Turn it into a plan

When the call wraps, ask:

Prompt
Turn this into a one page plan.

Not on Team or Enterprise?

Do this instead

09

Use any notetaker and my follow-up prompt

Claude Meetings needs a Claude Team or Enterprise plan. On any other plan, take the summary from whatever notetaker you use, or your own quick notes, and paste it into Claude or ChatGPT with my follow-up email prompt.
