5 ChatGPT SettingsTo change today

One to turn OFF, four to turn on. Click your name, then Settings.

Here are the 5 ChatGPT settings I'd change. Four you turn on. One you turn OFF, and that one matters most, so it's first.

All five live in Settings. On a computer, click your name in the bottom left corner, then Settings.

Turn this OFF

Stop ChatGPT training on your chats

01

Improve the model for everyone: OFF

Settings > Data controls > Improve the model for everyone. Switch it off. Yes, this still matters: on Free, Plus and Pro accounts it's on by default, which lets OpenAI use your chats, and anything your connected apps pull in, to train its models.

  • ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Edu workspaces aren't used for training by default.
  • Using Claude? Same idea. On Free, Pro and Max plans, go to Settings > Privacy and turn off the option that lets your chats be used to train models.

Turn these ON

The 4 to turn on

02

Custom instructions

Settings > Personalization. Turn on Enable customization, then fill in the Custom Instructions box. Tell it who you are, what you sell, who your clients are, and how you write. The box holds 1,500 characters on Free and Go, and 5,000 on paid plans like Plus. Here's a starter. Fill in the brackets and paste it in:

Prompt
I run [business name], which helps [who you serve] with [what you do]. My clients are usually [describe them]. When you write for me, keep it short, warm and direct, in plain English. If you need more detail, ask me a question instead of guessing. Never use em dashes.

03

Memory

Settings > Personalization > Memory. Turn on Reference saved memories so you stop repeating yourself. You can also turn on Reference chat history so it builds on past chats.

  • See what it remembers: click Manage in the Memory settings and delete anything that's wrong.
  • Want it to drop something? Just tell it: “Forget that I…”
  • Need one chat that ignores your memory? Start a Temporary chat and choose Unpersonalized.

04

Multi-factor authentication

Settings > Security and login > Multi-factor authentication (MFA). Turn it on and pick a method: an authenticator app, a push notification to a trusted device, a text message, or a passkey. Email isn't an option, so have your phone handy.

05

Connected apps

Settings > Plugins (older phone apps may call it Apps). Pick the app, click Install plugin, then Connect, and sign in. Start with the ones you use every day, like Google Drive, Gmail, or Outlook email and calendar, so it can work with your real files. Turn training off first (step 1), so what it reads stays out of training.

Menu names move around sometimes. If you can't find one, search for it in Settings.
