Here are my 3 starter prompts for Claude.

The prompts

Try all three

01

Projects

Create a project called [Your business]. Upload your offer, pricing and FAQs. Then ask:

Prompt
Using these files, write 5 answers to the questions my customers ask most.

02

Connectors

Go to Settings > Connectors and connect Google Drive, Calendar or HubSpot. Then ask:

Prompt
What's on my calendar this week and what do I need to prep?

03

Artifacts

In any chat, ask:

Prompt
Build me a client onboarding checklist for [your business] as an interactive checklist I can share with my team.
