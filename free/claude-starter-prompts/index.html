3 ClaudeStarter prompts

Projects, Connectors and Artifacts, step by step, with a prompt for each.

Here are my 3 starter prompts for Claude, one each for Projects, Connectors and Artifacts.

Do them in order. By the end, Claude knows your business, can see your week, and has built you something to share with your team.

Prompt 1: Projects

Teach Claude your business

01

Create a project for your business

On your computer, open the Claude desktop app or go to claude.ai. Click Projects in the left sidebar, then + New Project, and name it after your business. Projects work on every plan, including free (free accounts get up to 5).

02

Upload everything about your business

This project is Claude's reference library for your business, so give it everything a new hire would need on day one. In the project knowledge panel on the right, click + and add:

  • Your offer: what you sell, who it's for and what's included.
  • Your pricing: packages, rates and what costs extra.
  • Your FAQs: the questions customers ask and how you answer them.
  • Your website copy, sales pages or brochures.
  • The proposals, onboarding docs and welcome emails you send clients.
  • Your process: how a new customer gets from first call to done.

Before you upload: my never-paste list →

03

Tell Claude how to work

Click Set project instructions, paste this in, fill in the brackets, and save:

Prompt
I run [business name], which helps [who you serve] with [what you do]. Write in a [friendly, plain-English] tone. Use the files in this project for any facts about my offer, pricing or process. If the answer isn't in the files, say so instead of guessing.

04

Ask your first question

Start a new chat inside the project and ask the prompt below. Read every answer the way your customer would. If something's wrong, fix the file in the project, not just the chat, so Claude gets it right from then on.

Prompt
Using these files, write 5 answers to the questions my customers ask most.

Prompt 2: Connectors

Let Claude see your week

05

Connect your calendar and email

In Claude, go to Customize > Connectors and click + next to Connectors. Pick Google Calendar, click Connect, and sign in to give Claude access. Add Gmail and Google Drive the same way, plus your CRM if it's in the directory. On Microsoft 365 instead of Google? Check the directory for it. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner may need to turn connectors on first.

Claude's guide: Use connectors ↗

06

Turn them on in your chat

In a new chat, click the + button, hover over Connectors, and make sure the ones you just added are switched on.

07

Ask about your week

Then ask:

Prompt
What's on my calendar this week and what do I need to prep?

08

Go one step further

If you connected your email and Drive too, follow up with:

Prompt
For each meeting, find the related emails and files and give me a 3-line prep note: who it's with, what we last talked about, and what I need to bring.

Prompt 3: Artifacts

Build something you can share

09

Ask for a checklist

In any chat, ask the prompt below. Even better, ask it inside your business project from Prompt 1, so Claude builds it from your real process.

Prompt
Build me a client onboarding checklist for [your business] as an interactive checklist I can share with my team.

10

Make it yours

Claude builds it in a panel next to the chat. Ask for changes in plain English, for example:

  • “Add a step for sending the welcome email.”
  • “Group the steps by week.”
  • “Add who owns each step.”

11

Share it with your team

When it looks right, open the checklist, click Share, and choose Anyone with the link. Most shared checklists open without a Claude account. On Team and Enterprise plans, sharing stays inside your company unless an owner allows outside links.

Start at claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app.
