What's included

Six capabilities that take a deal from Opportunity to signed, all inside Salesforce.

Native quote generation

Generate professional quotes from existing Opportunity data, Products, custom fields, and attachments with a single click. Built-in versioning, revision history, and configurable expiration logic keep full audit control.

Embedded proposal builder

Create polished proposals alongside the quote in the same workflow. Add, remove, and reorder rich-text sections, insert imagery, customize individual line-item descriptions, and reuse templates for standard offerings.

Interactive buyer experience

Deliver secure, tokenized links by email. Prospects view quotes and proposals on an interactive web portal and can request changes or approve — with no logins, registration, or separate user licenses.

Legally binding e-signatures

Capture secure e-signatures directly on the buyer-facing page. On approval, the engine automatically generates a tamper-evident PDF archive with the signed document and a complete Certificate of Completion.

Buyer telemetry

Real-time engagement analytics written back to Salesforce: when a quote is opened or revisited, scroll depth, section time-allocation heat maps, and device telemetry — visibility for leadership and sales ops.

Salesforce-ready automation

Native data architecture lets quote actions trigger standard Flows, update Opportunity fields, route discount thresholds for internal approval, and send immediate email alerts on key status shifts.

The honest FAQ

Where does our data live?

In Salesforce. The engine is 100% native with zero external data storage, so quotes, proposals, and signed documents stay in your org, not in a third-party system.

Do buyers need a login or a license?

No. Prospects open a secure, tokenized link and can review, request changes, and sign with no logins, registration, or separate Salesforce user licenses.

Are the e-signatures legally binding?

Yes. Signatures are captured securely on the buyer-facing page, and each approval produces a tamper-evident PDF archive with a complete Certificate of Completion.

Do we need Salesforce CPQ?

No. This is built to fit your process and your existing licenses. If CPQ is genuinely the right tool for you we will say so; if it is overkill, we will build what you actually need.

How is the price set?

By the complexity of your pricing rules, approvals, and templates. You get the number before we start, and it does not move.
