The honest FAQ

Do we have to hire you to make the changes?

No. The map and the playbook are the product. Some clients ship the changes themselves, some hire us, some hire someone else. It is written so any competent team can act on it.

Is this safe?

Access is read-only and scoped. We review configuration and metadata, not your customer records, and access is revoked when we are done.

Does Salesforce not already show dependencies?

Where used shows some references, one component at a time, and misses plenty. This connects the full chain across flows, fields, automations, and integrations, and tells you what the risk actually is.

How is the price set?

By org size and complexity: automation volume, code, integrations. You get the number before we start, and it does not move.
