What's included

Seven strategic artifacts, delivered at the end of the engagement.

Revenue process map

Rep behavior findings

Data trust assessment

A direct read on how reliable your pipeline metrics actually are, and where the data integrity breaks down.

Fix-first roadmap

A prioritized, sequenced execution plan that tackles the highest-risk deficiencies before the standard operational annoyances.

Executive business case

Leadership readout session

A live, 90-minute working session with your leadership team to review the findings, walk the data models, and finalize next steps.

The honest FAQ

How much of our team's time does this take?

About six hours total across the four weeks, mostly access and a few confidential rep interviews. The audit is read-only, so your sales operations keep running uninterrupted.

Do we have to hire you to fix what you find?

No. The audit is the product. You get the roadmap and the business case, and you can execute them in-house, with us, or with someone else entirely.

Is this safe?

Yes. Access is read-only and scoped. We analyze your pipeline data and interview your team; we never change your configuration, and access is revoked when we are done.

How are the leak numbers calculated?

From your own historical CRM data. Every leak, stalled pipeline, lapsed renewals, lead-response failures, discount leakage, is quantified in data-verifiable dollars, not estimates.
